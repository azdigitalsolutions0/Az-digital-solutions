Information you provide

When you submit a website enquiry, we may collect your name, phone number, optional email address, selected service and message. When you submit a customer review, we may collect your name, city, service, star rating, review text and an optional photo.

Please do not submit passwords, payment details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information in a review or enquiry.

Website submissions are stored using Supabase, which provides the database and optional review-photo storage used by this website. Information may be processed by that provider to operate these features.
